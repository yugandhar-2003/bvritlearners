Instructions

Click on each section to learn more

About Video Tutorial
  • Click on the video tutorial button
  • You can find many programming related video tutorial cards
  • Select the programming language which you are willing to earn
  • Click on the explore button on card. You will be directed to a video page
  • You can see the videos in 3 languages. Click on the language which you are willing to learn
  • At top right corner of the video you can see the playlist button. You can listen to the whole tutorial by clicking on playlist
  • At bottom you can find the resources. You can see and download the PDF of tutorial here
  • You can see the PDF by BVRIT mail only (You cannot access through other mails)
Academic Videos
  • Click on the Academic videos
  • You can see all the branches in BVRIT
  • Allied branch and CSE students should see CSE session
  • Select the playlist
Programming Quiz
  • Click on the Programming Quiz button
  • You can see the quizzes
  • After completion of every quiz you can see number of attempts with score
Road Map Generator
  • Click on the Road Map Generator button
  • Select the programming language and enter the number between 1 to 24
  • You will get a schedule
About Question Papers
  • Click on the Question Papers button
  • You can see all year Question papers
  • Select the year. You can find the question papers on particular year
About Course Structure
  • Click on the Course Structure button
  • You can see all regulations
  • Select the regulation (R20/R20A/R22)
  • Find your branch and get the PDF
About BVRIT PDFs
  • Click on the BVRIT PDFs button
  • You can see all PDFs of each semester
  • Select the semester and branch
  • You can find PDFs of all units
  • You can see the PDF by BVRIT mail only (You cannot access through other mails)
About Text Books
  • Click on the Text Books button
  • You can see different cards of individual branches
  • Select the card (Select Your branch)
  • You can find common subject PDF on common Subjects card
  • You can see the PDF by BVRIT mail only (You cannot access through other mails)
About Website
  • You can access the resources (PDFs) only through BVRIT mail ID
  • You cannot access the resources through personal mail
  • All the PDFs are gathered from the subject teachers

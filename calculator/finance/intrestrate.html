What is Interest Rate?

Interest rate is the amount charged by lenders to borrowers for the use of money, expressed as a percentage of the principal... it can also be described alternatively as the cost to borrow money...

Simple vs. Compound Interest

There are two methods for calculating interest. Simple interest is calculated as a percentage of principal only, while compound interest is calculated as a percentage of the principal along with any accrued interest... This calculator deals with compound interest typical for loans.

Fixed vs. Variable Interest Rates

Fixed rates are rates that are set as a certain percentage for the life of the loan and will not change. Variable rates are interest rates that can fluctuate over time... This calculator will only display the result as a fixed interest rate.

APR

The interest rate for many types of loans is often advertised as an annual percentage rate, or APR... APR is a more accurate representation than the interest rate when shopping and comparing, as it includes certain fees (though this calculator only considers the principal, term, and payment).

Uncontrollable Economic Factors that Affect Interest Rate

  • Economic Policy and Inflation: Interest rates fluctuate mainly due to monetary policy...
  • Economic Activity: As interest rates go down, more businesses and people are inclined to borrow...
  • Unemployment Rate: Interest rates and unemployment rates are normally inversely related...
  • Supply and Demand: The market for credit is determined by supply and demand...

Controllable Factors that Determine Interest Rate

While many factors that affect the interest rate are uncontrollable, individuals can affect the interest rates they receive.

  • Individual Credit Standing: Credit scores represent creditworthiness...
  • Secured loans: Unsecured loans generally carry higher interest rates...
  • Loan specifics: Longer terms can increase the rate...
  • Do not apply for credit too often: Too many inquiries can lower scores...
  • Borrow at opportune moments: Borrow when economic factors are favorable...
  • Research and shop around: Different lenders have different rates...

Real Interest Rate

Auto Loans Explained (Summary)

Auto loans help finance vehicle purchases, typically over 36-84 months. Secured loans require monthly principal and interest payments. Missing payments can lead to repossession.

Financing Options

Direct lending (banks, credit unions) often provides more negotiation power than dealership financing (convenient, may have manufacturer offers like low APRs).

Fees & Costs

Beyond the price, expect Sales Tax (varies by state, sometimes reduced by trade-ins), Document Fees, Title/Registration Fees, Destination Fees, and mandatory Insurance. Decide whether to pay these upfront or finance them (check the box above).

Strategies

Prepare by determining affordability, research desired vehicles and rates, get pre-approved for loans, negotiate, and maintain good credit for lower interest rates. Consider cash rebates vs. low APR offers. Paying off loans early can save interest (check for penalties). Consider used cars or leasing as alternatives.
